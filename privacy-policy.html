Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026/06/26

1. Data Ownership & Infrastructure

We only provide the open-source code for CIPTER-MD. Your MongoDB database and VPS/Server infrastructure belong entirely to you. You retain full ownership and responsibility over all data processed by the bot. You can view our official license here: LICENSE.md.

2. Connection & Session Data

When you use our Connect page to generate a session, we temporarily store the generated session data to facilitate the setup process. However, this session data is automatically permanently deleted from our database within 24 hours (1 day). We do not collect any other personal information during the connection process.

3. Use of Data

We do not collect, store, or sell any of your personal messages, contacts, or chat data. The bot operates entirely on your own environment.

4. Code Obfuscation & Security

Portions of the CIPTER-MD source code are obfuscated strictly to protect our intellectual property. We guarantee that there is no hidden malware, viruses, or data tracking. If you have concerns, you can deploy the bot on your own local PC and monitor the network logs to verify that no unauthorized requests are being made.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the author via the official repository.
